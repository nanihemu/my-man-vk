Virat Kohli - Biography

About Virat Kohli

Virat Kohli is an Indian international cricketer known for his batting, consistency and performances across cricket formats.

Born on 5 November 1988 in Delhi, India, he rose through India's age-group cricket system.

He captained India's Under-19 team to victory in the 2008 Under-19 Cricket World Cup.

He made his international debut in 2008 and has represented India in Test, ODI and T20I cricket.

Kohli has also played in the Indian Premier League for Royal Challengers Bengaluru.
